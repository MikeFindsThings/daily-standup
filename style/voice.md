# Voice

## What the samples show

- One line. Usually under about 20 words.
- Proper capitalization and normal punctuation. Casual, but not sloppy.
- Deadpan. Never explain the joke, never add a takeaway.
- The joke reacts to the specific cause or detail of the news (a ground stop caused by a bad software upgrade becomes "never push directly to prod"), often from my own point of view. It is not generic tech humor that could apply to any story.
- Tie it to me or a relatable tech-worker moment when it fits (job hunting, prod deploys, AI bills), but the link to the actual story comes first.
- Inside-baseball tech references are good (push to prod, API bills), as long as a tech person gets it instantly.
- Understatement beats exaggeration.
- Any tech topic is fair game: AI, big tech, outages, product launches, dev culture, industry absurdity.

## Banned

Reject any draft that does one of these.

- Fake inspirational stories ("My 6-year-old asked me…", "A janitor taught me…", "I got rejected and here's what I learned").
- "Broetry": one short sentence per line, or runs of short punchy sentences.
- Insight-porn framing: "Here's the thing:", "Let that sink in", "This is huge", "Unpopular opinion:", "Agree?", "Thoughts?", or any engagement bait.
- Lessons, takeaways, or "what this means for leaders".
- Hashtags, emoji walls, em-dash-heavy AI prose.
- Hype words: game-changer, revolutionize, unlock, delve, landscape, journey, excited to announce.
- Anything longer than about two short sentences, or anything that explains the joke.
- Generic jokes that don't hinge on a specific detail of the story.
- Anything mean toward a specific private person, or anything that would embarrass me with a recruiter reading it.

## Post formats

1. **repost**: the story link plus my one-liner. The most common format.
2. **one-liner**: no link, just a topical observation that stands on its own (like the airport one).
3. **gif**: a GIF plus caption, ONLY when an image genuinely makes the joke land (like the cat on the keyboard). Most days, no GIF.
